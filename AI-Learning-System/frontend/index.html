What is the TCSSS AI Learning Platform?

The TCSSS AI Learning Platform is a free educational tool built for students of Tomia Community Senior Secondary School. It brings together nine interactive study tools — AI chat, quizzes, flashcards, theory practice, exam mode, visualisation, formula writing, study plans, and a mistake bank — inside one environment that works in any web browser.

The platform follows a simple learning cycle: Learn → Visualize → Practice → Analyze → Adapt. You start by asking the AI tutor a question, then visualise the concept with a diagram, practise with quizzes and flashcards, review your mistakes, and adapt what you study next based on how you did.

It is designed for Nigerian secondary school students preparing for WAEC, NECO, and JAMB exams, and follows the Nigerian senior secondary school curriculum. Every explanation is written in plain English, at a level students from JSS 1 to SSS 3 can follow.

What you can do

Nine study tools built for Nigerian secondary school students

Every tool is designed around the way students actually revise — short sessions, lots of practice, and clear feedback. Each tool is also available directly inside Chat, so you never have to leave a conversation to ask for a quiz or a diagram.

AI Chat Tutor

Ask any question in plain English. Get step-by-step explanations with real examples from Nigerian life. Available 24/7, patient, and never makes you feel bad for asking.

Quizzes

Generate auto-marked multiple-choice quizzes on any topic. Every answer comes with an explanation of why it's right or wrong. Take them in Chat or in the Lab.

Flashcards

Review key terms with spaced repetition. Cards you struggle with come back sooner, cards you know are spaced further apart — the fastest way to memorise definitions.

Theory Practice

Fill-in-the-gap questions that test recall exactly the way WAEC and NECO ask them. Every blank is graded instantly and shows the accepted answer.

Exam Mode

Take timed exams under real conditions — no hints, no second chances. Automatically submitted when time runs out, just like the real thing.

Visualizations

Turn any topic into a flowchart, mind map, or diagram you can actually see and remember. Especially useful for Biology cycles, Geography processes, and Physics concepts.

Sketch & Formula Studio

Write chemistry formulas and physics equations correctly. H₂SO₄, not H2SO4. CO₂, Ca(OH)₂, and every mathematical expression renders beautifully.

Study Plans

Generate a structured multi-day revision plan for any topic or exam. Tick off tasks as you complete them and watch your progress grow.

Mistake Bank

Every question you get wrong is collected automatically, grouped by topic, with the correct answer and an explanation. Revise only what you actually need to.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Everything students and parents ask before using the platform.

What is the TCSSS AI Learning Platform?

The TCSSS AI Learning Platform is a free educational tool built for students of Tomia Community Senior Secondary School. It combines an AI chat tutor, quizzes, flashcards, theory practice, timed exam mode, visualizations, formula writing, study plans, and a mistake bank in one place.

It works in any web browser — no app installation needed — and is designed for students preparing for WAEC, NECO, and JAMB exams.

Is the platform really free?

Yes. The platform is completely free for every TCSSS student. There are no ads, no subscriptions, and no hidden charges. It is built and maintained by the school's team as a free learning resource.

Who can use the platform?

All students of Tomia Community Senior Secondary School. Every student receives a personal account from the school. Accounts are private — each student sees only their own conversations, quizzes, flashcards, notes, and progress.

Do I need to install an app?

No. The platform runs entirely in your web browser. You can open it from any device — phone, tablet, or computer — by visiting the site and logging in with your school account. Nothing needs to be downloaded or installed.

Does the platform work on my phone?

Yes. The platform is designed mobile-first, meaning it works best on phones. Every page, quiz, flashcard, and chat works on small screens. You can also add the site to your home screen for one-tap access.

Is it useful for WAEC and NECO preparation?

Yes — exam preparation is one of the main reasons the platform was built. The Exam Mode tool lets you take timed exams under real conditions. Quizzes and theory questions match the style and difficulty of WAEC and NECO exams. The Mistake Bank tracks your weak topics so you can revise smarter.

Can it help me with JAMB?

Absolutely. JAMB uses multiple-choice questions across English, Mathematics, and your chosen subjects. The platform's quiz tool generates multiple-choice questions on any topic, and Exam Mode lets you practise under time pressure. Study Plans can be built around a JAMB timetable.

How does the AI tutor work?

The AI tutor answers questions, explains concepts, solves problems, and creates learning material on demand. It follows the same teaching style as a patient older student — simple language, clear steps, real examples. It is powered by a chain of AI providers that ensures reliable access 24/7.

Are my conversations private?

Yes. Your conversations are private to your account. Other students cannot see them. Only the school's administrator can view conversations for safety and support purposes, and every access is recorded in an audit log.

How do I report a problem or ask for help?

Open the Contact Us page from the sidebar of the platform. You can report a bug, request a feature, ask a question, or report a serious issue. Bullying reports are escalated immediately. For urgent matters, the page lists phone numbers of team members you can call.

Which AI providers power the answers?

The platform uses a chain of top AI providers with automatic fallback so answers keep coming even if one provider is unavailable. The system picks the best available model for each request.

Can I use the platform offline?

The platform needs an internet connection to talk to the AI. However, you can download books and PDFs from the Library to read offline. Quizzes you've already generated may remain accessible offline for a while.

Who built the platform?

The platform was built by a team of students and contributors from Tomia Community Senior Secondary School, led by Emmanuel Ajibade. See the Team section above for everyone who contributed.

How often is the platform updated?

The team actively develops the platform. New tools, fixes, and improvements are deployed regularly. Features are added based on student feedback collected through the Contact Us page.

Where can I find the school's official website?

The official website of Tomia Community Senior Secondary School is maintained by the school community at gideon-olukanni.github.io/TCSSS. The AI Learning Platform is a separate project, built by students for students.
