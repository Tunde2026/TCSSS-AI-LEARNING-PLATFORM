The AI Learning Platform

A free, all-in-one study environment for students of Tomia Community Senior Secondary School.

The AI Learning Platform is a free educational tool for students of Tomia Community Senior Secondary School (TCSSS). It combines AI chat, quizzes, flashcards, practice questions, diagrams, and study plans in one place so students can learn, practice, and prepare for WAEC and NECO exams from any device. The platform follows the learning cycle Learn → Visualize → Practice → Analyze → Adapt.

What you can do on the platform

Every tool is built for the way Nigerian secondary school students actually study.

Chat with an AI tutor

Ask any question in plain English. The tutor explains concepts, solves problems, and shows step-by-step reasoning.

Quizzes

Take auto-generated multiple-choice quizzes on any topic. Get instant scoring with explanations for every answer.

Flashcards

Review key terms and definitions using spaced repetition, so the cards you struggle with come back sooner.

Fill-in-the-gap theory

Practice recall with theory questions that test exactly what exams ask.

Exam Mode

Take timed exams under real conditions — no hints, no second chances, just like WAEC.

Visualizations

Turn any topic into a flowchart, mind map, or diagram you can actually see and remember.

Sketch / Formula Studio

Write chemistry formulas, physics equations, and math notation correctly. H₂SO₄ not H2SO4.

Spark

Discover your academic strengths through an adaptive assessment that measures reasoning, interests, and problem solving.

Frequently asked questions

Everything students and parents ask before they start using the platform.

What is the AI Learning Platform?

The AI Learning Platform is a free educational tool for students of Tomia Community Senior Secondary School (TCSSS). It combines AI chat, quizzes, flashcards, practice questions, diagrams, exam mode, and study plans into a single learning environment that works on any phone or computer.

Who can use the platform?

The platform is available to all students of Tomia Community Senior Secondary School. Every student receives a personal account from the school. Accounts are private — each student sees only their own conversations, quizzes, flashcards, progress, and mistakes.

Is the platform free to use?

Yes. The platform is completely free for TCSSS students. There are no ads, no subscriptions, and no hidden charges. It is built and maintained by the school's team as a free learning resource for students.

Do I need to install an app?

No. The platform runs entirely in your web browser. You can open it from any device — phone, tablet, or computer — by visiting the site's URL and logging in with your school account. Nothing needs to be downloaded or installed.

Does the platform work on my phone?

Yes. The platform is designed mobile-first, meaning it works best on phones. Every page, quiz, flashcard, and chat works on small screens. You can also add the site to your home screen for quick access.

What is Spark?

Spark is an adaptive assessment that helps students discover their academic strengths. It asks reasoning, interest, and problem-solving questions that change based on your answers, then shows you which subject areas — Science, Arts, or Commerce — currently align best with your profile. Spark is a guide, not a decision.

What is the Studying Lab?

The Studying Lab is the deeper learning workspace inside the platform. It contains nine tools: Notes, Flashcards, Quiz, Quiz History, Practice, Visualization, Study Plans, Exam Mode, and the Mistake Bank. Every one of these tools can also be used directly from Chat.

How does the AI tutor work?

The AI tutor answers questions, explains concepts, solves problems, and creates learning material on demand. It follows the same teaching style as a patient older student — simple language, clear steps, real examples. It is powered by a chain of AI models that provides reliable 24/7 access.

Are my conversations private?

Yes. Your conversations are private to your account. Other students cannot see them. Only the school's administrator can view conversations for safety and support purposes, and every access is recorded in an audit log. Admins cannot send messages as you or change your content.

How do I report a problem or ask for help?

Open the Contact Us page from the sidebar of the platform. You can report a bug, request a feature, ask a question, or report a serious issue. Bullying reports are escalated immediately. For urgent matters, the page lists the phone numbers of team members you can call directly.

Which AI model powers the answers?

The platform uses a chain of top AI providers — Groq, Cerebras, Google, NVIDIA, OpenRouter, and self-hosted Ollama — with automatic fallback so answers keep coming even if one provider is down. The platform picks the best available model for each request.

Can I use the platform offline?

The platform needs an internet connection to talk to the AI. However, you can download books and PDFs from the Library to read offline. Quizzes you have already generated can be taken offline for a while if your browser has cached the page.

Who built the platform?

The platform was built by a team of students and contributors from Tomia Community Senior Secondary School, led by Emmanuel Ajibade. The project is open source and lives on GitHub. See the Team section above for everyone who contributed.

How often is the platform updated?

The team actively develops the platform. New tools, fixes, and improvements are deployed regularly through the school's GitHub repository. Features are added based on student feedback and requests collected through the Contact Us page.

Where can I find the school's official website?

The official website of Tomia Community Senior Secondary School is maintained by the school community at gideon-olukanni.github.io/TCSSS. This AI Learning Platform is a separate project, built by students, for students.
