Questions & answers

Frequently asked

Everything students and parents ask before they start using the platform.

What is the AI Learning Platform?

A free educational tool for students of Tomia Community Senior Secondary School (TCSSS). It combines AI chat, quizzes, flashcards, practice questions, diagrams, exam mode, and study plans in one place.

Who can use the platform?

All students of TCSSS. Every student receives a personal account from the school. Accounts are private — each student sees only their own conversations, progress, and mistakes.

Is the platform free to use?

Yes. Completely free. No ads, no subscriptions, no hidden charges. It is built and maintained by the school's team as a free learning resource.

Do I need to install an app?

No. The platform runs entirely in your web browser. Open it from any phone, tablet, or computer by visiting the URL and logging in with your school account.

Does the platform work on my phone?

Yes. The platform is designed mobile-first. Every page, quiz, flashcard, and chat works on small screens. You can also add the site to your home screen for quick access.

What is Spark?

An adaptive assessment that helps students discover their academic strengths. It asks questions that change based on your answers, then shows which subject areas — Science, Arts, or Commerce — currently align best with your profile. Spark is a guide, not a decision.

What is the Studying Lab?

The deeper learning workspace inside the platform. It contains Notes, Flashcards, Quiz, Quiz History, Practice, Visualization, Study Plans, Exam Mode, and the Mistake Bank. Every tool is also usable from Chat.

Are my conversations private?

Yes. Your conversations are private to your account. Other students cannot see them. Only the school's administrator can view conversations for safety and support — every access is recorded in an audit log.

How do I report a problem or ask for help?

Open the Contact Us page from the sidebar. You can report a bug, request a feature, ask a question, or report a serious issue. Bullying reports are escalated immediately, and the page lists phone numbers for urgent matters.

Where can I find the school's official website?

The official website is maintained by the school community at gideon-olukanni.github.io/TCSSS. This AI Learning Platform is a separate project, built by students for students.

Ready to start learning?
